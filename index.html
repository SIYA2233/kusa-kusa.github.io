Still have a question? Talk to us ↗
How do I get started?

Send an enquiry and tell us a little about what you're looking for. A team member can guide you through the next steps and the information we'll need.

How much could I borrow?

It depends on your circumstances, the type of finance, and an assessment of affordability. The calculator provides an illustration only; our team can discuss what may be available where you live.

What will my repayments be?

Repayments depend on your approved amount, interest rate, loan term, fees and currency. The calculator is a starting point, not a quote. We'll explain any actual offer before you decide.

Can I apply from anywhere?

Availability and lending requirements depend on location. Get in touch to confirm whether Kusa Kusa can offer financing where you live.

Am I guaranteed approval?

No. Applications are reviewed individually, and all finance is subject to eligibility, affordability checks, and any applicable local requirements.
